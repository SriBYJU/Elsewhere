# What is distinctive about Elsewhere?

Elsewhere combines spatial educational simulations with bounded research, inspectable assumptions, reproducible branches, walk-through navigation, and a portable `.elsewhere` document. The distinction is the complete workflow: ask about a connected system, enter and manipulate a world, compare a frozen alternative, and inspect why an outcome changed. Interactive learning, visual causal models, procedural 3D worlds, and neural-network playgrounds have substantial prior art.

## Closest precedents

| Project | Established overlap | Elsewhere's intended distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PhET](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/about) | Research-based science and mathematics simulations, direct controls, multiple representations, immediate feedback | A common document and comparison workflow across urban, neural, and semiconductor worlds, with explicit claim provenance. Elsewhere does not claim PhET's educational research validation. |
| [TensorFlow Playground](https://playground.tensorflow.org/) | Editable neural networks, training data, learning rate, noise, activation and loss visualization in a browser | Neural training is one authored world inside a broader branching and evidence experience. Neural visualization itself is established prior art. |
| [LOOPY by Nicky Case](https://ncase.me/loopy/) | Drawing causal systems, asking what-if questions, remixing and challenging models | Domain-specific numerical models connect the causal view to equations, bounded parameters, source references and branch comparisons. |
| [Explorable Explanations](https://explorabl.es/) | A community of learning-through-play interactive explanations | A consistent workspace for saving and revisiting multiple authored models, rather than claiming the invention of explorable learning. |
| [Observable Framework](https://observablehq.github.io/framework/) | Reactive charts, inputs, reproducible data apps, and static deployment | An end-user world document and spatial manipulation workflow; users explore the supplied worlds without writing a notebook or app. |

Sources checked September 20, 2026. The right column is an interpretation of Elsewhere's design, not a claim that every competitor lacks every listed feature. This is a focused landscape review, not an exhaustive prior-art or patent search.

## Concrete product hypothesis

The potentially distinctive combination is:

1. A versioned `.elsewhere` document captures world identity, a strictly validated generated blueprint when needed, parameters, time, branch state, and history so an experiment can be reopened.
2. The reality compiler routes an authored match directly or maps another question through bounded public context and a disclosed archetype, while separating sources, assumptions, and simulation outputs.
3. Overview and human-scale walk-through modes share one current simulation; spatial manipulation and causal ripple make changes visible while numerical outputs remain inspectable.
4. A frozen branch and timeline comparison retain the alternative being tested.
5. WHY and challenge surfaces expose the model's explanation and limitations, encouraging users to question the representation.

The current scope includes three authored educational domains plus generic archetypes for companies, finance, supply chains, CPUs, the internet, science, history, energy, ecosystems, and other systems. The compiler can retrieve context for an arbitrary question, but it does not discover a scientifically valid causal model from unrestricted prose or turn public context into calibrated coefficients. Generated worlds are explicit exploratory hypotheses. The `.elsewhere` extension is a project format, not an established open standard, and imported documents remain data rather than general-purpose executables.

## How to test the differentiation

Run a comparison study using the same question in Elsewhere and the closest domain tool. Measure whether participants can identify an assumption, explain a changed metric, retain a baseline, and reopen their experiment. Collect task completion, explanation accuracy, and uncertainty recognition. Treat a polished interface as a design result; do not claim improved learning or scientific validity until these are measured.
